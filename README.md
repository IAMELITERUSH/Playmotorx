🚗 MotorX

DRIVE · EARN · COLLECT

MotorX is a browser-based driving simulator where you can explore a city, complete missions, earn rewards, collect cars, and drive with friends online.

🌐 Play now: https://playmotorx.netlify.app

⸻

🎮 Game Modes

* Free Drive – Explore the city at your own pace with no timers or objectives.
* Missions – Take on different jobs, including driving, deliveries, taxi work, and speed challenges.
* Levels – Progress through 25 levels, earn stars, and unlock bigger rewards.
* Multiplayer – Create online rooms or join friends using room codes.

🚘 Features

* Interactive driving experience
* Multiple cars and a garage
* Car dealership
* Missions and progression system
* Coins and rewards
* Gas stations and fuel management
* Properties and interactive city map
* Online multiplayer
* Autopilot feature
* Dynamic weather: clear, rain, and snow
* Day and night settings
* First-person and third-person camera views
* MPH and KM/H speed units
* Mobile-friendly driving controls

🕹️ Controls

Mobile

* Drag the steering wheel to steer.
* Hold GAS to accelerate.
* Hold BRAKE to slow down.
* Use the gear selector to change gears.

Keyboard

* WASD or arrow keys to drive.
* S or Space to brake.
* Hold the brake and press 1–4 to select P, R, N, or D.

🌐 Multiplayer

Play online with friends!

* Create a room and share its code.
* Join an existing room using a room code.
* Drive together in the same game world.

Multiplayer uses third-party relay servers. Avoid using your real name or personal information as your player name.
