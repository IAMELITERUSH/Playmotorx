MotorX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

MotorX ("the game") is a single-player and optional multiplayer driving game. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: we have no user accounts and no advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. We use one analytics service (Metricool) to count how many people play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, cars, homes, settings, and the player name you type for multiplayer) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress & delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game which items you bought so they can be unlocked, and you can restore them with Restore Purchases.

Multiplayer (optional)

Multiplayer only starts if you choose it and agree to the notice shown first. When you join or create a room, the game sends the following to the other players in that room: the player name you chose, the car and colour you selected, and your car's position and movement. There is no text or voice chat.

This information travels through third-party public relay servers (message brokers operated by Eclipse, EMQX, HiveMQ or similar services). Like any internet service, those relays can see your IP address and the messages passing through them. We do not control their logs or policies. We do not store multiplayer data. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name.

You can report or block any player from the room list, and the room host can remove players.

Usage statistics (Metricool)

The game loads an analytics script from Metricool (tracker.metricool.com), on both the website and the app version. When it loads, Metricool can receive your IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city is derived), your device, browser and operating-system type, the time of your visit, and a random identifier or cookie used to count unique visitors. We use this only to understand how many people play the game and from which regions. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not combined with data from other apps or websites, and does not include your name, email, game purchases or the player name you type for multiplayer. The script is not loaded when you are offline. You can switch it off any time in Settings → Anonymous usage statistics (takes effect the next time the game starts). Metricool's own privacy policy applies to how it handles this data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because other players in a multiplayer room are strangers who choose their own names, parents may wish to supervise multiplayer use by younger players.

Third parties

The third parties involved are Apple (purchases), Metricool (usage statistics, described above) and the relay servers used for optional multiplayer. The game contains no advertising software, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it using the setting above or remove the app. We run no servers and keep no personal data about you. The anonymous usage statistics are held by Metricool; to ask about them, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the game and on this page with a new date.
