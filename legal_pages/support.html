Quick answers

  • Restore a purchase: Settings (⚙️) → Restore Purchases, or the button at the bottom of the Coin Shop.
  • Report or block a player: in a multiplayer room, tap ⚑ (report) or 🚫 (block) next to their name.
  • Delete my data: Settings → Reset progress & delete my data.
  • Refunds: contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
